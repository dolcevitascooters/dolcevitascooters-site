03 / Your Quote Availability / Specification / Delivery
Before You Pay

Know what you are buying.

Once you have selected a scooter, contact us for a personalized quotation. We confirm the scooter, specification and delivery details before payment is arranged.

Your destination is also important. International shipping arrangements and applicable delivery costs are confirmed as part of the quotation so that you understand the agreed purchase and delivery terms before proceeding.

03

05 / After Payment Documentation / Restoration / Export
What Happens Next

Two paths. One destination.

What happens after payment depends on whether you have selected a scooter already completed and available in our inventory, or asked us to restore a vintage machine to your specification.

In both cases, our team coordinates the necessary preparation in Vietnam before the scooter is released for international shipment.

01
Scooter Already in Inventory

Ready scooter. Export preparation.

If you purchase a scooter that is already completed and available at our workshop, we move directly into the ownership transfer and export preparation process.

01

Ownership Transfer
We complete the applicable vehicle ownership transfer procedures for the buyer.

02

Registration Documents
We arrange the required procedures for deleting the scooter from its Vietnamese registration certificate for export.

03

Export Preparation
Once the required documentation is prepared, we coordinate the scooter's export process with our shipping forwarder.

04

Shipping Schedule
We book the available shipping schedule with our forwarder and prepare the scooter for international transport.

02
Custom Restoration

Your specification. Our workshop.

If you have ordered a custom-built vintage Vespa or Lambretta, restoration begins after payment has been received and the project requirements have been confirmed.

01

Restoration Begins
Our workshop begins the restoration according to the agreed model, colour, specification and restoration requirements.

02

Progress Updates
We send photographs during the restoration so you can follow the work as your scooter takes shape.

03

Finished Scooter
When restoration is complete, we photograph the finished scooter and send the final images to you for confirmation.

04

Ready for Export
After the finished scooter is confirmed, we proceed with the necessary export preparation and arrange international shipping.

DV

Restoration photographs are progress updates rather than a substitute for the final inspection. For custom orders, the finished scooter photographs provide the final visual confirmation before export preparation and shipping proceed.

06 / Shipping From Vietnam to Your Destination

Crated, cleared & shipped.

Once all required export documents are ready and the shipping schedule has been confirmed by our forwarder, we hand the scooter over to the forwarder for international shipment.

The forwarder handles the wooden crate packaging, export customs clearance and shipment arrangements so your vintage Vespa or Lambretta can leave Vietnam properly prepared for international transport.

01

Shipping Schedule Confirmed

After the export documentation is complete, our forwarder confirms the available shipping schedule and we arrange the handover of the scooter.

02

Handover to Our Forwarder

We deliver the scooter to our appointed forwarder, who takes responsibility for the next stages of packaging and export shipment preparation.

03

Wooden Crate Packaging

The scooter is prepared and secured inside a wooden shipping crate for international transport, with protection appropriate for the journey.

04

Export Customs Clearance

Our forwarder handles the export customs clearance and the required shipment procedures before the consignment leaves Vietnam.

05

On Board

Our usual shipping schedule has the consignment going on board on Sunday each week, subject to the forwarder's confirmed sailing schedule.

Optional Insurance

Protect your shipment with insurance.

Customers may choose to purchase shipping insurance at an additional cost. If you would like insurance, let us know when confirming your shipping arrangements so the applicable cost and coverage can be included in your quotation.

Shipping Details

For more information about international shipping, wooden crating, delivery, destinations and shipping terms, please visit our Shipping page ↗ or contact us directly.

07 / Delivery Arrival at Your Destination

From the crate to your door.

Once the scooter has been exported from Vietnam and shipped to its destination, the final stage is delivery according to the shipping arrangement confirmed in your quotation.

For door-to-door shipments, the forwarder coordinates the onward delivery to the agreed destination address. Delivery arrangements and local procedures can vary depending on the destination country.

01

Arrival at Destination

The consignment arrives in the destination country and proceeds through the applicable import and delivery procedures.

02

Door-to-Door Delivery

Where door-to-door delivery is included in your confirmed shipping arrangement, the forwarder coordinates delivery to the agreed address.

03

Receive the Wooden Crate

When the scooter arrives, inspect the wooden crate and its exterior condition before opening it. We recommend taking photographs or video if you notice any visible damage.

04

Uncrate & Inspect

Carefully open the crate and check the scooter, accessories and visible condition against the agreed specification and final photographs.

05

Ready for the Road

Your vintage Vespa or Lambretta has completed its journey from our workshop in Vietnam and is ready for the next chapter with its new owner.

At Delivery

Check the shipment before opening.

If the crate or packaging shows visible signs of transport damage, document the condition clearly with photographs and contact us as soon as possible. This documentation can be important when handling a shipping or insurance claim.

Shipping Details

08 / After Delivery Registration & Ongoing Support

Delivered isn't the end.

Receiving your scooter is only the beginning of ownership. Once the scooter has arrived, we provide the full set of legal export and vehicle documents required for you to proceed with registration in your country.

Using these documents, the new owner can complete the local registration process and obtain the appropriate registration and license plate according to the requirements of the destination country.

01

Receive the Legal Documents

Dolce Vita Scooters provides the full set of legal documents prepared for the export and transfer of the scooter.

02

Register Your Scooter

The owner uses the supplied documentation to complete the registration process and apply for a new license plate with the relevant authority in the destination country.

03

Ride & Enjoy

Once local registration and any required road-legal procedures are complete, your restored vintage scooter is ready to be enjoyed on the road.

04

Stay Connected With Us

Your relationship with Dolce Vita does not end when the scooter leaves Vietnam. Our team remains available to support you throughout ownership, including questions that may arise during use of your scooter.

Warranty & Support

We stay with the scooter after delivery.

Every scooter is covered by our applicable warranty, and our support continues after delivery. If you have questions about the scooter, its components, operation, maintenance or ownership, you can contact us and our team will assist wherever we can.

Beyond the Sale

We believe buying a vintage scooter should be a long-term relationship, not simply a transaction. From the first inquiry to life with the scooter on the road, Dolce Vita Scooters remains available to support our customers.

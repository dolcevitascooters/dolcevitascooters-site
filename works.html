03 / In-Depth Restoration Process Frame · Engine · Components
A machine is restored piece by piece.

Disassemble.
Inspect.
Restore.

A proper restoration begins long before paint is applied. Each vintage Vespa or Lambretta is completely disassembled so that the frame, engine and individual components can be examined on their own terms.

The objective is simple: preserve what is original and structurally sound, repair what can be saved, and replace what time has genuinely worn beyond service.

01
The Frame

Back to bare metal.

The scooter is completely stripped before restoration begins. Rubber, plastic, leather, aged aluminium trim, electrical wiring and harnesses are removed so the original structure can be properly examined.

The frame is taken back to bare metal and inspected for cracks, previous repairs, structural damage and alignment. Where necessary, it is repaired and adjusted before moving into the body and paint process.

02
Paint & Finish

Colour is part of the machine's identity.

Once the frame has been repaired and aligned, the bodywork is prepared for paint. A primer coat is followed by multiple layers of high-quality paint, carefully applied to achieve a durable finish while respecting the character of the original model.

03
Engine Rebuild

The original engine remains at the heart of the scooter.

The engine is cleaned and inspected for cracks, stripped threads and bearing-surface tolerances. Pistons, rings, carburetors, bearings and clutch components are replaced where required, while the gears and clutch assembly are rebuilt and tested as part of the restoration.

04
New Where Necessary

Replace what time has taken.

A vintage scooter cannot be preserved simply by keeping every old component. Aged cylinders, cylinder heads, exhaust systems, rubber hoses, brake shoes, shocks, springs, seats, tires, cables, locks and electrical components are replaced where their condition requires it.

The majority of these replacement components are new, purpose-made parts for Vespa and Lambretta scooters, selected to support reliability without compromising the identity of the original machine.

04 / Components New Parts, Where Needed
Replace / Retain

New parts, where needed.

A faithful restoration does not mean replacing everything. We retain sound original components and replace aged, worn or unreliable parts where necessary.

Throughout the restoration, each component is inspected before a decision is made. Parts that have reached the end of their useful life are replaced with carefully selected components suited to the original Vespa or Lambretta.

From mechanical components and electrical parts to rubber, cables, brakes, suspension, tyres, seats and other aged details, the goal is reliability without losing the character of the original machine.

06 / Continuous Improvement Atelier Standards
Methods / Materials / Standards

We refine the process.
And protect the result.

Restoration is never a finished formula. We continue to refine our methods, materials and inspection standards as we learn more from every machine.

We pay close attention to the components that matter most to the reliability and character of a vintage scooter. Fuel tanks, engine components, electrical parts and other aged items are evaluated carefully before being replaced.

Where replacement is necessary, we source components from trusted manufacturers and suppliers, including selected Italian-made parts. Fuel tanks, for example, are properly prepared, primed and finished before installation.

Engine components such as pistons, cylinders, cylinder heads and crankshafts are selected with the same attention to quality and compatibility. Every scooter is monitored through its restoration and checked before it leaves the workshop.

02

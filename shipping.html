02 / Payment & Shipping Prepared in Vietnam
Payment / Preparation / Dispatch

Prepared for
the journey.

From payment to departure, every scooter is prepared carefully before it leaves our workshop in Vietnam.

We accept payment by bank wire transfer or debit and credit cards through third-party payment processors. Full payment must be received before shipment.

Our scooters are shipped weekly, with departures scheduled every Sunday. Shipping documents are sent to the customer's address according to the shipping method selected, generally either within two weeks or one week before the scooter's estimated arrival.

Before dispatch, each scooter is thoroughly cleaned and inspected. Fuel is drained from the tank as required for safe transportation, and the machine is carefully prepared for international transit.

03 / Preparation for Transit Protection / Crating / Compliance
Clean / Protect / Secure

Packed for
the road ahead.

Before a scooter leaves Vietnam, we prepare it carefully for the conditions of international transport.

01 Before Packing

Clean & Inspect

Each scooter is thoroughly cleaned and inspected before it is prepared for shipment. Fuel is drained from the tank as required for safe transportation.

02 Paint Protection

Wrap & Protect

The scooter is wrapped in protective bubble wrap to help safeguard the paint and finished surfaces from dirt, scratches and contact during transit.

03 Custom Crating

Secure in Wood

The scooter is placed securely inside a custom-made wooden crate with protective padding on all sides to reduce movement during transportation.

04 International Transit

Ready for Export

We oversee the packing process and ensure the machine is properly prepared before it begins its international journey. Depending on the destination, fumigation may also be required to comply with local regulations.

In the unlikely event that your scooter arrives damaged, the condition should be documented immediately with the handling agent.

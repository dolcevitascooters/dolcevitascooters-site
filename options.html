Accessories Vespa & Lambretta

Accessories selection.

We offer a wide variety of retro Vespa and Lambretta accessories and parts to purchase with or without a new vintage scooter.

Many accessories can be shipped separately and are designed for straightforward installation by the owner. We can also provide guidance and instructions to help you personalize your scooter and choose the right accessories for your machine.

All new scooter orders automatically include a free set of spare parts including brakes, cables, spark plugs, a mixture cup and floor mat, with a value of $150.

No drilling or modifications Many accessories are designed to install without drilling or permanent modifications.

Front Rack

A folding front rack for carrying extra belongings, made from high-quality stainless steel that will not rust. It is easy to install by hooking onto the leg shield without drilling.

The rack fits easily onto our offered scooter models. Please check with us for compatibility with other models.

Separate order $99

Rear Rack 3IN1

A practical upgrade that complements our vintage scooters. Please check with us for compatibility with other models.

Separate order $175

Vinyl Seats

High-quality vinyl seats available in two different styles for four vintage Vespa models: VBA, VBB, VBC and VLB.

Turn Signals

Turn signals were not an original feature on many vintage scooters of the 1960s and 1970s, but they can be a useful modern upgrade for riding in busy city streets.

Signals can be positioned in the handlebars or on the front of the frame. Please note that installation requires adjustment to the frame, which can make returning the scooter to its original configuration more difficult.

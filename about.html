The original comes first.

A vintage Italian scooter is valued not simply for how it looks, but for what remains of the machine it once was.

We begin with the original machine wherever possible: its frame, engine, mechanical identity and surviving details. These are not incidental parts of a restoration. They are the foundation of its character.

03 / The Work Faithful restoration
Restoration is not reconstruction.

We restore what time
left behind.

Every scooter begins with a careful assessment of the machine as it is - not as we wish it to be.

The scooter is completely disassembled so the frame, engine and individual components can be inspected. Frames are taken back to bare metal where necessary, repaired, checked for alignment and prepared for their next life on the road.

Engines are rebuilt, aged or worn components are replaced, and sound original parts are retained whenever their condition allows. The objective is not to make an old scooter look new. It is to return a historic machine to a dependable, coherent and beautiful condition while respecting what makes it original.

01

Disassemble

The machine is dismantled so its true condition can be understood.

02

Inspect

Frame, engine and components are examined before restoration begins.

03

Restore

Structural, mechanical and cosmetic work is carried out with attention to the original machine.

04

Preserve

Original character and sound components are retained wherever possible.

04 / Provenance Vietnam · Italian machines
The history of a machine matters.

Italian scooters, another
chapter in Vietnam.

Many vintage Italian scooters found in Vietnam have lived more than one life before arriving at our workshop.

Over the decades, Vespa and Lambretta scooters entered Vietnam through different owners and circumstances. Some were brought in from Europe and Italy, while others changed hands through generations of local ownership.

Because Vietnam has experienced wars and major changes in administration over the history of these machines, original historical paperwork is not always available. We do not obscure that part of a scooter's story. Provenance is assessed from the machine itself, its identifying details and the documentation that can be established.

01

What we document

Before export, each scooter is prepared with current Vietnamese documentation required for its legal export from Vietnam.

We identify the machine as accurately as possible, document its restoration and provide the relevant paperwork for the next stage of its journey.

A collector's principle

A missing historical document does not automatically erase a machine's history. What matters is being transparent about what is known, what can be documented, and what remains part of the scooter's story.

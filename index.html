02 / The Original Frame — Engine — Provenance

Before restoration, there is the machine.

The original
comes first.

A vintage Italian scooter is valued not simply for how it looks, but for what remains of the machine it once was.

We look first for scooters with their original frames and engines, then preserve the character that makes each machine individual.

Where historical paperwork is no longer available, we do not invent a story. Provenance is documented as carefully as the machine itself, together with the current Vietnamese government documentation required for export.

03 / The Work Restoration — Preservation — Craft

Restoration is not reconstruction.

We restore
what time left behind.

Every scooter begins with a careful assessment of the machine as it is - not as we wish it to be.

Frames are inspected, repaired and aligned. Engines are rebuilt. Worn components are replaced where necessary, while sound original parts are retained whenever they can continue their life.

The aim is not to erase age, but to return the machine to the road with its character intact.

04 / The Journey Vietnam — Worldwide

From our workshop to your door.

The road
doesn't end here.

Your scooter leaves Vietnam ready for the next chapter of its life, with the journey arranged from our door to yours.

Each machine is prepared for international transport and secured in a custom wooden crate before shipment. We coordinate the export, international transport and destination delivery as one continuous journey.

Our quoted door-to-door total is designed to include applicable shipping, import procedures, duties, taxes, port and local delivery charges, so there are no unexpected transport costs waiting at the other end.

Privacy Policy Dolce Vita Scooters

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how Dolce Vita Scooters collects, uses and protects information provided through our website.

This policy applies to information collected through www.dolcevitascooters.com and does not apply to information collected offline or through third-party websites and services.

01

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you choose to provide when you contact us, submit an inquiry, request information about a scooter, or otherwise communicate with Dolce Vita Scooters through our website.

This information may include your name, email address, telephone number, shipping or destination information, and any other information you choose to include in your message.

Our website may also collect certain technical and usage information automatically, such as browser type, device information, IP address, referring website and general information about how visitors interact with our website.

02

How We Use Information

We use information provided through our website primarily to communicate with you and respond to your requests.

  • Respond to scooter and restoration inquiries.
  • Provide information about available scooters, restoration services and shipping.
  • Prepare quotations and respond to purchase or delivery requests.
  • Communicate with you regarding an existing inquiry or order.
  • Maintain, operate and improve our website and services.
03

Email Communications

If you contact us by email or through our website, we may use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry and continue communication regarding your request.

We will not use your contact information for unrelated purposes without a reasonable basis or your consent where consent is required.

04

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand how visitors use the site, and improve the user experience.

Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website functions may be affected if cookies are disabled.

05

Website Analytics and Technical Data

Like many websites, our website may collect technical information automatically when you visit. This may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring page and general navigation information.

This information may be used for website administration, security, troubleshooting, performance analysis and improving our services.

06

Third-Party Services

We use third-party service providers to support certain functions of our website. In particular, our website uses Formspree to process and deliver inquiries submitted through our online contact form.

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, the information you provide is transmitted to Formspree for processing and may be stored within the Formspree service before being delivered to us.

Formspree may process information such as your name, email address, destination, scooter preferences and message, depending on the information you choose to provide through our form.

Formspree maintains its own privacy and security practices. For more information about how Formspree handles personal information, please review its privacy policy and security information.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services. Information submitted directly to those services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

07

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our website, process inquiries, respond to your request, provide services, arrange shipping or otherwise conduct our business.

We may also disclose information when required to do so by law, legal process, or a lawful governmental request, or when necessary to protect our rights, property, users or the public.

08

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information provided to us through our website and to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, use or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or stored by us.

09

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us through our website, please contact us so that we can take appropriate steps.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or applicable practices.

When we make changes, the updated policy will be published on this page. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically for the latest version.

Privacy Questions

Need to ask us?

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Dolce Vita Scooters.
